#### 0:45 - 1:45 | Scene 2: The Solution Architecture & Pre-Alert Innovation
* **Visual on Screen:** Architecture Diagram and Mobile App Screen (`screenshot_citizen_guard.png` and `screenshot_citizen_countdown.png`).
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "Rakshak-AI is an autonomous, end-to-end emergency orchestration network that closes this entire loop in seconds.
  > First, our mobile sentinel app continuously buffers 50Hz sensor data. When a crash occurs, instead of waiting 10 or 15 seconds like existing apps, Rakshak-AI transmits an instant 'Pre-Alert' to the police control room the exact millisecond of impact. This gives first responders a 10 to 15-second head start.
  > Second, we prevent false alarms: our algorithm checks for free-fall signatures before impact. If you drop your phone, it detects the 0g free-fall and suppresses the alarm. But in a real crash, it measures speed drops and rollovers to calculate an evidence-based Crash Severity Index."
