### How to Submit to Google Drive:
1. Upload this entire `Vikist Blueprint` folder to your team's Google Drive.
2. Right-click the folder in Google Drive -> Click **Share** -> Change general access to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
3. Copy the Google Drive folder links into your main Idea Submission Form:
   * **Subfolder 1 (Concept Document):** Share link to `1. CONCEPT/CONCEPT_DOCUMENT.docx`
   * **Subfolder 2 (Pitch Files):** Share link to `2. PITCH FILES/`
   * **Subfolder 3 (Video):** Share link to your recorded 5-minute video (or upload your video into `3. VIDEO/`)
